How Much Energy Does a Television Use?

Televisions consume electricity whenever they are operating. The amount of electricity used can depend on factors such as screen size, display technology, brightness settings, operating time and energy efficiency.

A television that is used for several hours every day can contribute to household electricity consumption over time. Understanding these factors can help users make more informed choices.

Understanding Television Technologies

LED Televisions

LED televisions use an LED-backlit LCD display. Energy consumption can vary depending on the model, screen size and settings.

OLED Televisions

OLED televisions use individual pixels that produce their own light. Their energy consumption can vary depending on the content displayed and brightness.

QLED Televisions

QLED televisions use LCD technology with quantum-dot technology. Energy consumption varies between different models.

Tips for Reducing Television Energy Use

  • Turn the television off when nobody is watching.
  • Avoid unnecessarily high brightness settings.
  • Use available energy-saving modes.
  • Consider the television's energy rating when purchasing a new model.
  • Reduce unnecessary standby operation where practical.
